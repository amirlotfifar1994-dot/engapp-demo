It has the most extensive recent connection audit: 324 anchors, 14 families, five levels, six first-person characters and reviewed links into learning activities. Recent published work verified its desktop/mobile branch paths and Listening/Practice/Speak/Talk connections. The demo includes that version and the shared progressive-card rollout.

- **Park10003:** reconstructed detail work was integrated from the standalone lesson. The source records granular learning, sentence models, activity integration and desktop/mobile reviews.
- **Home10004:** 258 nested observations across a reconstructed household scene, with all five language levels and shared learning/activity surfaces. It preserves the detailed home work already present in the demo and gains the current core.
- **Museum10010:** 211 observations and four connected viewpoints demonstrate a larger connected scene while retaining the same learning activities. All required selected scene assets are included.

The source references are its Garden connection review, Cafe connected-tour review and Park integration notes, plus the current selected lesson packs and native manifests. These source documents are private authoring evidence and are not copied into this repository. Beach/post-office checkpoints and unfinished agent work are not included.

This is a reasoned selection from the reviewed published material, not a claim that every pixel, microphone, voice or all lessons in the private application have been exhaustively certified. Current demo-specific verification and its limits are in REVIEW.md.
